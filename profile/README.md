<h2 align="center">Boreasward · 溯风</h2>

<p align="center"><em>Against the wind, northward.</em><br><sub>逆风，向北。</sub></p>

<p align="center">
Boreas is the north wind. <em>-ward</em> means toward.<br>
Boreasward is walking into the wind, to where it begins.<br>
溯风：顶着风走回风的来处。
</p>
